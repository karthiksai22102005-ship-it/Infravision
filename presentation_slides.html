The Municipal Dilemma

Problem Statement & Background Crisis

Traditional municipal road maintenance is slow, manual, fragmented, and vulnerable to contractor fraud.

🚨 Dangerous Road Hazard Delays

  • Manual Inspection Bottlenecks: Visual surveys are infrequent, allowing potholes and fractures to deteriorate unchecked.
  • High Accident Toll: Unaddressed potholes cause thousands of severe vehicular damage cases and fatal road accidents each year.
  • Exponential Repair Costs: A 10mm surface crack neglected for weeks degrades into structural base failure costing 10x more to repair.

📑 Bureaucratic Triage Deficits

  • Fragmented Complaints: Citizen reports get trapped in paper logs, siloed WhatsApp groups, or unresponsive web portals.
  • Duplicate Clutter: One central pothole receives dozens of redundant complaints with zero spatial aggregation.
  • Lack of SLA Enforcement: No real-time tracking of work orders, leading to neglected complaints in underprivileged wards.

💸 Contractor Fraud & Zero Audit

  • Substandard Resurfacing: Unregulated contractors dump uncompacted gravel and claim full hot-mix asphalt payouts.
  • No Verifiable Proof: Supervisors lack objective photographic alignment or surface roughness data to certify work.
  • Budget Waste: Billions of municipal tax dollars are drained on recurrent repairs of the exact same road stretch.
Autonomous Operations

Proposed Solution: 6-Stage Autonomous Lifecycle

A closed-loop, self-orchestrating pipeline managing defects from computer vision trigger to certified closure.

1. DETECT

On-Device CV: YOLOv8 scans video frames at 60 FPS for potholes, cracks & debris.

2. LOCATE

GPS & GIS: Precision coordinate lock, altitude, and road network segment binding.

3. PRIORITIZE

AI Severity: Computes Hazard Index (0-1) and launches dynamic SLA timers (24h/48h).

4. ASSIGN

Smart Triage: Routes tickets to PWD, Civil, Electrical, or Drainage wings.

5. REPAIR

Ghost Camera: Crew resurfaces road guided by transparent original photo overlay.

6. VERIFY

Forensic Seal: AI verifies 0% residual cavity and stamps cryptographic closure.

🎯 SAE Autonomy Level 4 Architectural Standard

InfraVision achieves SAE Level 4 (High Autonomy) in civil asset management: The system handles detection, prioritization, department assignment, and repair verification autonomously under defined municipal operational parameters. Human supervisors only intervene for edge-case resolutions and budget payouts, completely removing manual triage bottlenecks.

🗺️ Geospatial & Security

Deep Technology

Key Technical Innovations & Algorithms

Proprietary mathematical models and vision algorithms driving enterprise-grade reliability.

⚡ 100% Local Edge Inference

Zero cloud latency (22.1ms avg) and zero cloud API subscriptions. Eliminates server downtime and dead-zone blind spots during road sweeps.

📐 Hazard Severity Index (HSI)

Multi-factor severity scoring formula:

S = 0.25·C + 0.40·(D/D_max) + 0.35·(A/A_norm)

Automatically triggers 24h, 48h, or 7-day dynamic SLA countdown clocks based on hazard severity.

👻 Guided Ghost Viewfinder

Solves forensic angle mismatch. Superimposes original pre-repair damage at 40% opacity in contractor's viewfinder to guarantee identical perspective and focal distance for audit certification.

🔒 Cryptographic Evidence Chain

SHA-256(Raw Bytes || Timestamp || GPS)

Generates an immutable 256-bit hash for every piece of media, preventing evidence tampering or invoice fraud.

🔍 Forensic Split-Wipe Studio

Interactive split-slider UI analyzing Surface Roughness (IRI < 1.5 mm/m), Compaction Density (99.4%), and GPS geofence match (<0.6m) before releasing green AI verification stamp.

🏢 3D Campus & City GIS Engine

Custom map renderer supporting 4 layers: Dark Vector, Satellite Recon, Defect Heatmap, and 3D Isometric building polygon extrusions without cloud map subscription costs.
