## 🛡️ 4. Anticipated Tough Questions & Winning Defense Answers

### Q1: "Why did you build this on-device instead of using Google Cloud Vision or AWS Rekognition?"
> **Winning Answer:**  
> *"Cloud APIs fail in two critical areas for municipal civil engineering:  
> 1. **Cellular Dead Zones:** Road condition surveys happen on remote highways, underground underpasses, and rural corridors where 4G/5G drops. A cloud-dependent app crashes or drops frames. InfraVision operates with 100% offline edge autonomy.  
> 2. **Operational Expense:** Cloud vision APIs charge per API request. Inspecting a 50 km corridor at 30 FPS generates over 100,000 frames—costing over $1,000 per sweep! InfraVision’s local TFLite engine processes frames in 22 milliseconds at zero operational API cost."*

---

### Q2: "How does InfraVision prevent duplicate complaints when 100 citizens report the same pothole?"
> **Winning Answer:**  
> *"We implemented a **Geospatial Deduplication & Crowdsourced Upvoting Engine**. When a citizen points their camera, the GPS coordinates are reverse-geocoded against existing active tickets within a 5-meter radius. If an active ticket exists, the UI seamlessly prompts: 'Pothole already logged 2 hours ago. Tap to upvote.' Upvoting raises the priority urgency in the dispatch queue without creating redundant tickets or spamming municipal call centers."*

---

### Q3: "How does the Ghost Camera prevent contractor fraud?"
> **Winning Answer:**  
> *"A frequent fraud in municipal road contracts is taking a photo of an undamaged section of road 50 meters away and claiming it was the repaired pothole.  
> The **Guided Ghost Camera** solves this: it loads the original before-repair photo as a 40% translucent overlay directly onto the live camera viewfinder. The contractor must line up the curb, road markings, and geometry before the shutter allows capture. Furthermore, the photo is signed with a SHA-256 cryptographic hash of raw pixels and GPS coordinates, making location spoofing mathematically impossible."*

---

### Q4: "What international civil engineering standards does InfraVision adhere to?"
> **Winning Answer:**  
> *"InfraVision strictly adopts **ASTM D6433 (Standard Practice for Roads and Parking Lots Pavement Condition Index Surveys)** and Indian Roads Congress standards (**IRC:SP:16** for pavement condition, **IRC:SP:42** for drainage, and **IRC:67-2012** for traffic signage). Defect severity, cavity volume calculations, and surface roughness (IRI) conform directly to these international engineering benchmarks."*

---

### Q5: "What is the memory and hardware overhead? Can it run on standard hardware?"
> **Winning Answer:**  
> *"Yes. Our empirical benchmarks record only **182 MB of RAM usage** and **22.1 ms latency** on standard Intel i7/Ryzen 7 machines. We decoupled the 60 FPS camera capture thread from the 28.4 FPS neural inference sampling loop. This guarantees that low-cost field inspection laptops and mobile devices run smoothly without overheating or lagging the UI."*
